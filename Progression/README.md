Add-on for inserting a versatile progress bar into the main Deck Browser, Deck Overview, and Reviewer Anki windows.

Daily card data is calculated respecting deck limits, current new and due counts, and future (re)learning steps. The add-on allows for a **fine control over presentation of each category** and **infinite customization options**. The progress bar can interact with card templates and [merge seamlessly into their individual design](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/memrise-card-template-support-thread/34233/355?u=eltaurus).
